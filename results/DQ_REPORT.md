### `counts_latest_day_complete` (⚠️ warn)

The newest day has at least 90% of the rows of a usual day (median of the previous 28). If not, "yesterday" totals are partial.

1 failing of 1 checked (100.00%).

First 1 failing records:

| sensing_date | rows_on_newest_day | median_rows_previous_28_days | pct_of_usual |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-18 | 354 | 2273.0 | 15.6 |

<details><summary>Query</summary>

```sql
with daily as (
    select sensing_date, count(*) as n_rows from counts group by sensing_date
), newest as (select max(sensing_date) as day from counts), usual as (
    select median(n_rows) as median_rows
    from daily, newest
    where sensing_date between newest.day - 28 and newest.day - 1
) select daily.sensing_date, daily.n_rows as rows_on_newest_day,
       usual.median_rows as median_rows_previous_28_days,
       round(100.0 * daily.n_rows / usual.median_rows, 1) as pct_of_usual
from daily, newest, usual where daily.sensing_date = newest.day and daily.n_rows < 0.9 * usual.median_rows
```

</details>
